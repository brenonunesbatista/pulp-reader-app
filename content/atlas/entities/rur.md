Karel Čapek wrote *R.U.R.* (Rossum's Universal Robots) in 1920; it opened on 2 January 1921 in Hradec Králové and at
the National Theatre in Prague on 25 January [1]. A factory makes artificial workers; they revolt and wipe out
humanity, though a new future begins with the survivors [1]. Čapek's robots are not machines but artificial
biological beings that can be mistaken for humans [1].

The word "robot" was suggested by his brother Josef, from the Czech *robota*, forced labour [1]. The play reached
New York in October 1922 and had been translated into thirty languages by 1923 [1].
