Mamoru Oshii's film premiered on 18 November 1995, adapting Masamune Shirow's 1989 manga [1]. Major Motoko Kusanagi
hunts an artificial intelligence called the Puppet Master in the New Port City of 2029, in a story about
consciousness and identity; Kenji Kawai's score draws on traditional Japanese music [1]. The Wachowskis showed it to
their producer while preparing *The Matrix* [1].

Where the robot story becomes a question about the soul, in animation.
