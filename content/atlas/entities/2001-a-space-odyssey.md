Stanley Kubrick's film opened in April 1968; he wrote it with Arthur C. Clarke, starting from Clarke's stories "The
Sentinel" (1951) and "Encounter in the Dawn" [1]. On the way to Jupiter, the ship's computer HAL 9000, with its
human-like voice, turns against the crew [1]. Kubrick used existing music — Richard Strauss, Johann Strauss II,
Ligeti — and won his only Oscar, for the visual effects [1].

## Why it matters
The machine that thinks, calmly, against us: HAL is still the reference for AI on screen.
