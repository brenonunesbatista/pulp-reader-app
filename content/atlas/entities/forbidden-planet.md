Released by MGM on 23 March 1956, with Walter Pidgeon, Anne Francis, Leslie Nielsen — and Robby the Robot [1]. Its
story has often been compared to Shakespeare's *The Tempest* [1]. Louis and Bebe Barron wrote cinema's first
completely electronic score [1]. It influenced *Star Trek* and was added to the National Film Registry in 2013 [1].

## Why it matters
A polite, Three-Laws-style robot steps onto the screen, to an electronic soundtrack.
