The first issue of the first science fiction magazine [1]. It opened with Hugo Gernsback's editorial "A New Sort of
Magazine" and reprinted Jules Verne, H. G. Wells ("The New Accelerator") and Edgar Allan Poe [1][2]. The cover is by
Frank R. Paul [1].

## Why it matters
The day science fiction got its own newsstand slot. It is readable in Banca.
