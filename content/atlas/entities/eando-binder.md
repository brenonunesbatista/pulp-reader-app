"Eando Binder" — from "E and O" — was the pen name of the brothers Earl Andrew Binder and Otto Binder [1]. Their
story "I, Robot" (*Amazing Stories*, January 1939) introduced the robot Adam Link, who returned in a series of
sequels in the early 1940s; by then Otto did most of the writing [1].

Otto Binder went on to write thousands of comic-book stories, for Captain Marvel and Superman among others, and
co-created Supergirl [1].

## Why it matters
A pulp writer whose sympathetic robot reached Isaac Asimov, and who carried science fiction into comics.
