Ridley Scott's film, released on 25 June 1982 with Harrison Ford, Rutger Hauer and Sean Young, follows a blade
runner hunting escaped replicants in the Los Angeles of 2019 [1]. It disappointed at the box office, then grew into
one of the most admired science fiction films; Vangelis wrote its synthesizer score [1]. Scott's own *Final Cut*
came in 2007, and *Blade Runner 2049* followed in 2017 [1].
