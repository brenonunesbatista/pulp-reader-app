Published by Doubleday in 1968 and set in a San Francisco devastated by nuclear war, where real animals are rare
status symbols [1]. The bounty hunter Rick Deckard must "retire" six escaped Nexus-6 androids, using the Voigt-Kampff
empathy test to tell them from humans [1]. Dick said in 1972 that our environment was becoming "quasi-alive" in ways
analogous to ourselves [1].

## Why it matters
The question shifts from "will machines rebel?" to "what makes us human?".
