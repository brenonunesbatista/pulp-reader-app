Karel Čapek was a Czech writer and playwright who lived from 1890 to 1938 [1]. His play *R.U.R.* (1920) introduced
the word "robot", which he credited to his brother Josef, a painter and writer; it comes from the Czech *robota*,
forced labour [1][2]. He also wrote the satirical novel *War with the Newts* (1936) [1].

An outspoken anti-fascist, he helped found the Czechoslovak PEN Club and was nominated for the Nobel Prize in
Literature seven times without winning [1]. He died of pneumonia on 25 December 1938, months before the Nazi
occupation of Prague [1].

## Why it matters
Every robot in this Atlas carries a word he made famous, and his robots were already a warning about labour and power.
