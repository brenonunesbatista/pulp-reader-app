Masamune Shirow's manga began in 1989 [1]. Its heroine is the cyborg agent Major Motoko Kusanagi [1]. Mamoru Oshii adapted it as the animated film *Ghost in the Shell*
(1995) [1].

## Why it matters
Cyberpunk in comics form: the body becomes hardware, and the self a question.
