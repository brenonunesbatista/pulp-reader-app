In "Runaround" (1942) Asimov stated the Three Laws of Robotics in full for the first time: a robot may not injure a
human or allow one to come to harm; it must obey humans unless that conflicts with the First Law; and it must protect
itself unless that conflicts with the first two [1]. Asimov credited John W. Campbell with putting the laws into words
during a conversation on 23 December 1940 [1].

The story was collected in *I, Robot* (1950) [1].

## Why it matters
The Three Laws still frame how people talk about the ethics of robots and AI.
