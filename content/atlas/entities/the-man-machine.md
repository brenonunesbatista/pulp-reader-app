Kraftwerk's seventh album came out on 19 May 1978 on Kling Klang in Germany and Capitol elsewhere [1]. Its singles
included "The Robots" and "The Model"; the cover by Karl Klefisch was inspired by El Lissitzky [1]. Critics see it as
a foundation of synth-pop [1].

## Why it matters
The robot as a sound: the music of machines, made by people pretending to be machines.
