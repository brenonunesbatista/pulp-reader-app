Developed by David S. Goyer and Josh Friedman, it premiered on Apple TV+ on 24 September 2021, with Jared Harris as
Hari Seldon and Lee Pace as Brother Day [1]. It departs freely from the books: its "genetic dynasty" of cloned
emperors — Brother Day, Brother Dusk and Brother Dawn — was invented for the show [1]. Three seasons have been
released (2021, 2023, 2025) and a fourth was announced in September 2025 [1].

## Why it matters
Asimov's psychohistory, finally on screen eighty years after the first story.
