The Dartmouth Summer Research Project on Artificial Intelligence, held at Dartmouth College in the summer of 1956
(roughly 18 June to 17 August), is widely considered the founding event of AI as a field [1]. Its organizers — John
McCarthy, Marvin Minsky, Nathaniel Rochester and Claude Shannon — introduced the term "artificial intelligence" in
their proposal of 2 September 1955 [1].

## Why it matters
The name "artificial intelligence" was born here, the same year Robby the Robot reached cinemas.
