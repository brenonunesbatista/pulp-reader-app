Francis Flagg's first published story, in *Amazing Stories* for November 1927 [1]. The narrator, Mr. Matthews, a
sixty-year-old widower writing a book against Marxist economics, sees a flash in his sitting room: where his rocking
chair stood there is a glass cylinder about five feet high, holding "a caricature of a man — or a child" [2]. The
magazine introduced it with the question "What will humanity look like 30,000 years hence?" [2].

## Why it matters
An early pulp vision of the human body merged with its machine — and you can read it in Banca.
