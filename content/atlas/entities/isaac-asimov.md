Isaac Asimov was born around 2 January 1920 in Petrovichi, Soviet Russia, arrived in the United States in 1923 and
died in New York on 6 April 1992 [1]. His first published story, "Marooned off Vesta", appeared in *Amazing Stories*
in March 1939; he was paid $64 for it [1]. He earned a PhD in chemistry from Columbia in 1948 and taught biochemistry
at Boston University [1].

Under John W. Campbell at *Astounding* he wrote "Nightfall" (1941), the robot stories collected in *I, Robot* (1950)
and the *Foundation* stories, whose trilogy won the Hugo Award for Best All-Time Series in 1966 [1][2]. He wrote or
edited around 500 books, many of them popular science [1].

## Why it matters
Asimov turned robots from monsters into characters with rules, and history into science fiction.
