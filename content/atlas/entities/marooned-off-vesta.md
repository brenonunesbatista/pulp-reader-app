Asimov wrote it in July 1938, at 18; *Astounding* turned it down and *Amazing Stories* published it in March
1939 [1]. Three survivors of the wrecked spaceship *Silver Queen*, with one spacesuit and three days of air, escape by
using the ship's water tank as a rocket to reach the asteroid Vesta [1]. A sequel, "Anniversary", appeared with it in
*Amazing Stories* in March 1959 [1].

The March 1939 issue is in Banca's catalog, but the Internet Archive has no scan of it yet.
