Directed by Fritz Lang from a screenplay by Thea von Harbou, *Metropolis* premiered in Berlin on 10 January 1927 and
is one of the first feature-length science fiction films [1]. Brigitte Helm plays both Maria and the
*Maschinenmensch*, the robot built in her image by the inventor Rotwang [1].

In 2001 it became the first film inscribed in UNESCO's Memory of the World; footage found in Buenos Aires in 2008
allowed a 2010 restoration close to the original length [1]. The robot's design later influenced C-3PO [1].

## Why it matters
The first great screen robot — and the image of the machine-woman that cinema keeps returning to.
