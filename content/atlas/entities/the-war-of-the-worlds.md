Serialized in 1897 in *Pearson's Magazine* (UK) and *Cosmopolitan* (US) and published as a book by William Heinemann
in 1898 [1]. Martians in tripod fighting machines armed with heat-rays attack southern England; the invasion fails
only when the Martians die of earthly germs to which they have no immunity [1].

It is one of the earliest stories of a conflict between humanity and an alien race, and has been adapted for radio,
film, music and television ever since [1]. *Amazing Stories* reprinted it in two parts in August and September
1927 [1][2].

## Why it matters
The template for every alien invasion story, and one of the most adapted novels ever written.
