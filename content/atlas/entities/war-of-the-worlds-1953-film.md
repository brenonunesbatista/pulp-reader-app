The first film of Wells's novel, made by Paramount with Gene Barry and Ann Robinson, moved the story to 1950s
Southern California [1]. Its Martian machines became manta-ray shaped craft instead of tripods [1]. It won the
Academy Award for visual effects and was added to the National Film Registry in 2011 [1].

## Why it matters
The Cold War version of the invasion, and a landmark of 1950s science fiction cinema.
