Osamu Tezuka's manga ran in Kobunsha's *Shōnen* from 1952 to 1968 [1]. Its hero is an android boy built by Dr. Tenma
to replace his dead son, later raised by Professor Ochanomizu [1]. The 1963 television series by Mushi Production was
the first popular Japanese animated TV series and shaped the look of anime; NBC Enterprises brought it to American
audiences [1]. The manga has sold over 100 million copies [1].

## Why it matters
Japan's answer to the robot: not a threat but a child, and the start of anime as we know it.
