Published by Gnome Press on 2 December 1950, it gathers nine stories first printed between 1940 and 1950 in *Super
Science Stories* and *Astounding* [1]. They are framed as the memories of the robopsychologist Susan Calvin, told to a
reporter [1]. The first story, "Robbie", appeared in 1940 as "Strange Playfellow" [1].

The 2004 film *I, Robot* borrowed the title, characters and the Three Laws, but its plot is largely original [1].

## Why it matters
The book that made the Three Laws famous.
