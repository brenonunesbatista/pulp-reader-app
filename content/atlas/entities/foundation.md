Asimov proposed the idea to John W. Campbell on 1 August 1941; the stories ran in *Astounding* from 1942 and were
collected by Gnome Press on 30 August 1951 [1]. Modelled on the fall of the Western Roman Empire, it follows the
mathematician Hari Seldon, whose psychohistory predicts the collapse of the Galactic Empire, and the Foundation he
sets up on the planet Terminus to shorten the dark age that follows [1].

The original trilogy won the Hugo Award for Best All-Time Series in 1966 [1].

## Why it matters
History as a science, on a galactic scale: the idea behind decades of space epics.
