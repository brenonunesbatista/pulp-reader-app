Launched by the Soviet Union from Baikonur on 4 October 1957, Sputnik 1 was a 58 cm metal sphere with four antennas,
the first artificial Earth satellite [1]. Its radio beeps could be heard by amateurs around the world for about three
weeks; it burned up on 4 January 1958 [1]. It set off the Space Race and led the United States to create NASA [1].

## Why it matters
Space travel left the pulp pages and entered the news.
