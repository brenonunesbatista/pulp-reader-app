*Amazing Stories* first appeared in April 1926, published by Experimenter Publishing and founded by Hugo Gernsback:
the first magazine devoted solely to science fiction [1]. Its first issues reprinted Jules Verne, H. G. Wells and
Edgar Allan Poe, and Frank R. Paul painted its covers [1].

Gernsback lost the magazine when his company was forced into bankruptcy on 6 March 1929 [1]. Ziff-Davis bought it in
1938 and hired Raymond A. Palmer as editor; in March 1939 it published Isaac Asimov's first story [1]. Its letter
column put isolated readers in touch with each other and laid the foundations of science fiction fandom [1].
