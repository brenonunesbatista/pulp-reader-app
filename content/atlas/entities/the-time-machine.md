First serialized in *The New Review* from January to May 1895 and published as a book that May [1]. A Victorian
scientist travels to the year 802,701 and finds humanity split into two species: the gentle Eloi above ground and the
Morlocks below, who feed on them [1]. Modern readers often see the two species as Victorian class division taken to
its end [1].

The book is credited with popularizing time travel by machine, and Wells coined the term "time machine" [1].
*Amazing Stories* reprinted it in May 1927 [2].

## Why it matters
Every time machine in fiction since is a descendant of this one.
