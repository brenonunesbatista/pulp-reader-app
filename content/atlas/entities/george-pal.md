George Pal produced *The War of the Worlds* (1953), the first film of Wells's novel, which won the Academy Award for
its visual effects [1]. In 1960 he produced and directed *The Time Machine* for MGM, which won the Academy Award for
Best Special Effects for its time-lapse sequences [2]. Before that he was known for his stop-motion animation of the
1940s [2].

## Why it matters
Pal brought Wells to the cinema twice, in the decade when science fiction films boomed.
