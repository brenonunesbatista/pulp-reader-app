Released by MGM on 17 August 1960, with Rod Taylor as the time traveller and Yvette Mimieux as Weena [1]. It won the
Academy Award for Best Special Effects for its time-lapse shots of the world changing as the traveller moves through
time [1]. The machine itself, designed by Bill Ferrari and built by Wah Chang, became an icon [1].

## Why it matters
The image of a time machine most people carry in their heads comes from this film.
