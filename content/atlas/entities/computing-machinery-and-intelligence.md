Alan Turing published the paper in the journal *Mind* in October 1950 [1]. Instead of asking "Can machines think?"
directly, he proposed the imitation game — now called the Turing test — in which a judge converses in writing with a
person and a machine and tries to tell them apart [1]. It is a founding text of the philosophy of artificial
intelligence [1].

## Why it matters
The moment the question of thinking machines moved from fiction to science.
