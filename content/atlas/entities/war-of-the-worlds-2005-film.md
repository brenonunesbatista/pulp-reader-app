Directed by Steven Spielberg from a script by Josh Friedman and David Koepp, with Tom Cruise and Dakota Fanning, and
narration by Morgan Freeman; it opened widely on 29 June 2005 [1]. The tripods return, rising from underground, as a
father tries to get his children to Boston [1]. Spielberg said it was the first alien film he made "where there is no
love and no attempt at communication" [1]. It earned about $604 million worldwide [1].

## Why it matters
Wells's invasion retold for a world shaped by 9/11-era fear.
