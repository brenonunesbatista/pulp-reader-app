For the August 1927 issue, which began a two-part reprint of *The War of the Worlds*, Frank R. Paul painted the
cover [1][2]. Martian tripods with heat-rays stride over a burning town while riders and crowds flee below, under the
line "Stories by H. G. Wells" (the cover itself, in Banca). Paul's huge machines shaped how early readers pictured
the future [2].

## Why it matters
Thirty years before the films, this is what a Martian invasion looked like.
