Francis Flagg was the pen name of George Henry Weiss (1898–1946), a poet, writer and novelist born in Halifax, Nova
Scotia [1]. His first published story, "The Machine Man of Ardathia", appeared in *Amazing Stories* in November 1927;
his science fiction also ran in *Astounding*, *Tales of Wonder* and *Weird Tales* [1].
