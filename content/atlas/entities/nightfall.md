Published in *Astounding Science Fiction* in September 1941 [1]. John W. Campbell gave Asimov the idea, quoting
Emerson — "If the stars should appear one night in a thousand years, how would men believe and adore" — and arguing
that people would go mad instead [1]. On the planet Lagash, lit by six suns, darkness comes once every 2,049 years,
and the sight of the stars destroys a civilization [1].

In 1968 the Science Fiction Writers of America chose it as the best science fiction short story written before
1965 [1].

## Why it matters
The story most often named as the best science fiction short story ever written.
