Released by CBS on 9 June 1978, Jeff Wayne's double concept album retells the novel through progressive rock,
orchestra, narration and song [1]. Richard Burton narrates, with Justin Hayward, David Essex, Phil Lynott and Julie
Covington among the singers [1]. It reached number one in 11 countries and has sold an estimated 15 million copies;
live tours began in 2006 [1].

## Why it matters
Proof that a Victorian novel can become a rock opera — and a best-seller.
