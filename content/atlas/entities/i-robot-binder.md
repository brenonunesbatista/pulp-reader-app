Published in *Amazing Stories* in January 1939, the story is the confession of Adam Link, a robot built and educated
by Dr. Charles Link [1]. When his maker dies in an accident, the housekeeper believes the robot killed him; hunted,
Adam reads *Frankenstein*, understands the prejudice against made beings, and chooses to confess rather than kill [1].

Isaac Asimov said he began "Robbie" two months after reading it, and his publisher later borrowed Binder's title for
Asimov's collection, over Asimov's doubts [1]. It was adapted twice for *The Outer Limits* (1964 and 1995), with
Leonard Nimoy in both [1].

The January 1939 issue is in Banca's catalog, but the Internet Archive has no scan of it yet.

## Why it matters
The sympathetic robot that set Asimov writing.
