Released on 26 October 1984, written by James Cameron with Gale Anne Hurd and made for $6.4 million [1]. Arnold
Schwarzenegger plays a cyborg assassin sent back in time to kill Sarah Connor (Linda Hamilton), whose son will lead
the fight against Skynet, a hostile artificial intelligence [1]. It earned about $78 million and was added to the
National Film Registry in 2008 [1].

## Why it matters
*R.U.R.*'s robot revolt, updated for the computer age — and still the shorthand for fear of AI.
