Published in *Astounding Science Fiction* in March 1944, with details of a uranium-235 bomb that John W. Campbell had
gathered from unclassified journals [1]. Military counterintelligence noticed the parallels with the secret work at
Los Alamos, and the FBI questioned Cartmill and Campbell [1]. As fiction it was weak — Cartmill himself called it
"that stinker" — but it became a famous footnote in the history of the bomb [1].

## Why it matters
The moment the science fiction magazines got too close to real secrets.
