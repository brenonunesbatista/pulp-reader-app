*Eerie* was Warren's second black-and-white horror magazine, launched in 1966 and hosted by Cousin Eerie [1]. In the
1970s it ran serialized characters such as Hunter and Exterminator, with many Spanish artists from the Selecciones
Ilustradas agency [1][2].
