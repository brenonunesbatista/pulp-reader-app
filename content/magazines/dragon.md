TSR launched *The Dragon* in June 1976 to succeed its newsletter *The Strategic Review* [1]. For three decades it was
the official magazine of *Dungeons & Dragons* — new rules, classes, monsters, Ed Greenwood's first Forgotten Realms
articles, fiction and comics such as *Knights of the Dinner Table* — and one of the best-selling gaming magazines ever
[1]. Paizo published it from 2002 until the last print issue in 2007; it continued online until 2013 [1].
