Todd McFarlane created *Spawn* in 1992 as one of the first titles of Image Comics, the company founded by artists who
left Marvel to own their work [1]. Its first issue sold about 1.7 million copies; the story of Al Simmons, a murdered
agent returned from Hell, became the longest-running creator-owned American comic, passing issue #300 in 2019 [1].
