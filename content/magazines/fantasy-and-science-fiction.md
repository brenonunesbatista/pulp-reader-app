Founded in 1949 by Anthony Boucher and J. Francis McComas — its first issue was called *The Magazine of Fantasy* —
*F&SF* set out to publish science fiction and fantasy as literature, with no interior illustrations and an emphasis on
style [1]. It printed Daniel Keyes's "Flowers for Algernon", Walter M. Miller's *A Canticle for Leibowitz* stories and
Stephen King's first Dark Tower tales, and it is still published today [1].
