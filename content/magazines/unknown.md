*Unknown* (later *Unknown Worlds*) was John W. Campbell's fantasy companion to *Astounding*, published from 1939 until
the wartime paper shortage ended it in 1943 [1]. It brought rational, witty fantasy to the pulps: Fritz Leiber's first
Fafhrd and Gray Mouser stories, L. Ron Hubbard, L. Sprague de Camp and Fletcher Pratt's Harold Shea, and Theodore
Sturgeon [1].
