*A Espada Selvagem de Conan* was Editora Abril's Brazilian edition of Marvel's black-and-white magazine *The Savage
Sword of Conan* [1][2]. Over 205 issues it brought Robert E. Howard's barbarian to Brazilian readers in the stories of
Roy Thomas, John Buscema, Alfredo Alcala and their successors [2].
