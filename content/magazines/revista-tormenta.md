*Revista Tormenta* was a magazine devoted to *Tormenta*, the Brazilian fantasy world born in *Dragão Brasil* [2].
Published by Editora Talismã in the *D&D* 3rd edition era, its eighteen issues (0–17) expanded the kingdom of Arton
with adventures, characters and rules for *3D&T* and *D&D* [1].
