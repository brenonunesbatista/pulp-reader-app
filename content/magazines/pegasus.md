*Pegasus* was the bimonthly magazine of Judges Guild, the Illinois publisher of the first licensed *Dungeons &
Dragons* supplements and the Wilderlands setting [2]. Its twelve issues brought adventures, maps and articles for
Judges Guild's campaigns and for fantasy role-playing in general [1].
