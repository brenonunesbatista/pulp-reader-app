Street & Smith's *The Popular Magazine* began in 1903, aimed at boys and soon at adults, and became one of the leading
general-fiction pulps, appearing twice a month at its peak [1]. It published H. Rider Haggard, Rafael Sabatini,
Dashiell Hammett and many adventure and western writers [1].
