*Blue Book* began in 1905 as *The Monthly Story Magazine* and became one of the best-paying, best-written pulps [1].
Edited for decades by Donald Kennicott, it published Edgar Rice Burroughs, Agatha Christie, Nevil Shute and many
adventure writers; it later turned into a men's magazine [1].
