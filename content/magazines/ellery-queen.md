*Ellery Queen's Mystery Magazine* was founded in 1941 by Frederic Dannay, one of the two cousins who wrote as Ellery
Queen [1]. Mixing new stories with rediscovered classics, it became the leading mystery fiction magazine, and it is
still published [1].
