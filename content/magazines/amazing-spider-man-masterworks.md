*Marvel Masterworks* is Marvel's long-running series of collected editions reprinting its classic comics in order [1].
The *Amazing Spider-Man* volumes begin with Stan Lee and Steve Ditko's *Amazing Fantasy* #15 and *The Amazing
Spider-Man* #1 (1962–1963) and follow the series through the John Romita years and beyond [1][2].
