Bob Kane and Bill Finger's Batman first appeared in *Detective Comics* #27 in May 1939, a pulp-inspired detective in a
cape, and had his own title from 1940 [1]. Here are his Golden Age stories and the landmark books of later decades:
Frank Miller's *The Dark Knight Returns* (1986), which made the hero grim and old [2]; *Year One* (1987), Miller and
David Mazzucchelli's retelling of his first year [3]; *A Death in the Family* (1988), where readers voted by phone on
the fate of Robin [4]; *Knightfall*, *The Long Halloween* [5] and *Hush*.
