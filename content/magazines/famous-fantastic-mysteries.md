*Famous Fantastic Mysteries*, edited by Mary Gnaedinger, reprinted classic fantasy and science fiction — first from
the old Munsey pulps, later novels by A. Merritt, William Hope Hodgson, Lord Dunsany and others [1]. It ran from 1939
to 1953 and is remembered for the illustrations of Virgil Finlay and Lawrence Sterne Stevens [1].
