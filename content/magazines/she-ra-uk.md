She-Ra, He-Man's twin sister Adora, starred in Filmation's *She-Ra: Princess of Power* (1985), made to bring the
Masters of the Universe to girls [1]. London Editions gave her a British comic in 1986–1987, scanned by the fans of
he-man.org [2].
