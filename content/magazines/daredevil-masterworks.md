The *Daredevil* volumes of *Marvel Masterworks* reprint the blind lawyer-hero's comics in order from *Daredevil* #1
(1964), created by Stan Lee and Bill Everett, through the 1960s and 1970s runs of Wally Wood, John Romita, Gene Colan
and others [1][2].
