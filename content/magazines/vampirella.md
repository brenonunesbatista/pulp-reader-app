*Vampirella* began in 1969 with a costume designed by Trina Robbins, a story by Forrest J Ackerman and a famous cover
by Frank Frazetta [1]. The magazine mixed her serial with horror stories and became one of Warren's best sellers until
the company closed in 1983 [1][2].
