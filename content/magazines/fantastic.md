Ziff-Davis launched *Fantastic* in 1952 as a slick, digest-sized fantasy companion to *Amazing Stories* [1]. Under
Cele Goldsmith (1958–1965) it published the first story by Ursula K. Le Guin and early work by Roger Zelazny, and
Fritz Leiber's Fafhrd and the Gray Mouser returned in its pages [1]. Ted White edited it in the 1970s before it was
merged into *Amazing* in 1980 [1].
