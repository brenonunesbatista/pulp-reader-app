*Miracleman* is the 1950s British hero Marvelman, revived by Alan Moore in 1982 as a dark, realistic study of what a
superhuman would mean for the world [1]. Eclipse Comics published the series in the United States from 1985: Moore's
story ran to issue 16, after which Neil Gaiman took over [1].
