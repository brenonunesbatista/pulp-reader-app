Stan Lee and Steve Ditko's Spider-Man first appeared in *Amazing Fantasy* #15 in 1962 and got his own title, *The
Amazing Spider-Man*, the next year [1]. This shelf follows a reading order collected on the Internet Archive: 585
books, from the Masterworks of the 1960s through the clone saga, *Civil War* and *Avengers vs. X-Men* to 2025 [2].
They keep that order; the years shown are estimated from the single issues along the way [2].
