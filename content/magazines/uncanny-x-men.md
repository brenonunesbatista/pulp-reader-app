*The X-Men* was launched by Stan Lee and Jack Kirby in 1963: teenage mutants trained by Professor X [1]. Sales were
modest and from 1970 the title reprinted old stories, until a new international team arrived in 1975 and Chris
Claremont began a sixteen-year run as writer [1]. With John Byrne he wrote *The Dark Phoenix Saga* (1980) and *Days of
Future Past* (1981), two of the most influential superhero stories [2][3]. The title became *Uncanny X-Men* in 1978
[1].
