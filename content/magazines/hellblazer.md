*Hellblazer* followed John Constantine — the chain-smoking English occultist Alan Moore created in *Swamp Thing* — for
300 issues from 1988 to 2013, the longest-running title of DC's Vertigo line [1]. Its writers included Jamie Delano,
Garth Ennis, Warren Ellis, Brian Azzarello, Mike Carey and Peter Milligan [1]. These scans carry a Portuguese
translation.
