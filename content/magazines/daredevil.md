Daredevil, the blind lawyer Matt Murdock, was created by Stan Lee and Bill Everett in 1964 [1]. Frank Miller turned
the series into urban noir around 1980 [1]. Here are two of his later stories: *Born Again* (1986), drawn by David
Mazzucchelli, in which the Kingpin learns Daredevil's identity and destroys his life [2], and *The Man Without Fear*
(1993), a retelling of his origin drawn by John Romita Jr. [3].
