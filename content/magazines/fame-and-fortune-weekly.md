*Fame and Fortune Weekly*, subtitled "Stories of Boys Who Make Money", was a dime-novel weekly from the New York
publisher Frank Tousey [1]. Each 32-page issue told a complete rags-to-riches tale of a young man winning success in
business, a late flowering of the cheap story papers that thrilled readers before the pulps took over [1][2].
