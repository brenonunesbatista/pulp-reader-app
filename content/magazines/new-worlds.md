*New Worlds* was Britain's leading science fiction magazine [1]. Under John Carnell (1946–1964) it nurtured British
writers such as Arthur C. Clarke, Brian Aldiss and J. G. Ballard; under Michael Moorcock from 1964 it became the
flagship of the experimental "New Wave" [1].
