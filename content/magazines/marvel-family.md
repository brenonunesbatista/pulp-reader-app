*The Marvel Family* brought Captain Marvel, Mary Marvel, Captain Marvel Jr. and their allies together in one title
from 1945; its first issue introduced their enemy Black Adam [1][2]. It ended in 1954, when Fawcett stopped publishing
Captain Marvel after DC's long copyright lawsuit [2].
