*Famous Monsters of Filmland*, edited by Forrest J Ackerman and published by James Warren from 1958, celebrated horror
and science-fiction films with photos, puns and Basil Gogos's painted covers [1]. It inspired a generation of
filmmakers and writers, from Steven Spielberg to Stephen King [1].
