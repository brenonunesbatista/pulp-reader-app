*Rod Serling's The Twilight Zone Magazine* was launched in April 1981 with the blessing of Carol Serling, six years
after her husband's death [1]. Edited first by T. E. D. Klein, it mixed new horror and dark fantasy fiction — by
Stephen King, Harlan Ellison, George R. R. Martin and many newcomers — with film coverage, interviews and the scripts
of classic episodes [1]. It ran until 1989 [1].
