*Isaac Asimov's Science Fiction Magazine* first appeared in 1977, with Asimov lending his name and an editorial to
each issue while George H. Scithers edited it [1]. Under Gardner Dozois (1986–2004) it became the most honoured
magazine in the field, winning many Hugo Awards for its stories [1]. Now *Asimov's Science Fiction*, it is still
published [1].
