*Weird Tales* was founded in 1923 and became the great pulp of horror and the fantastic, above all under editor
Farnsworth Wright (1924–1940) [1]. It published H. P. Lovecraft's Cthulhu stories, Robert E. Howard's Conan, Clark
Ashton Smith, C. L. Moore and the young Robert Bloch and Ray Bradbury, with covers by Margaret Brundage and Hannes Bok
[1]. The original run ended in 1954; the title has been revived many times since [1].
