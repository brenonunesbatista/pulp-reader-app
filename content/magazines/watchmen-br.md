Alan Moore, Dave Gibbons and John Higgins's *Watchmen* (DC Comics, 1986–1987) imagines costumed heroes in a real Cold
War world, and is often named the greatest graphic novel; it won a Hugo Award in 1988 [1]. This is a Brazilian
Portuguese edition in four volumes [2].
