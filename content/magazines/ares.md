*Ares* was the science fiction and fantasy sister of SPI's wargaming magazine *Strategy & Tactics*: every issue came
with a complete game, plus fiction and articles [1]. Launched in 1980, it passed to TSR when TSR acquired SPI in 1982
and ended in 1984 [1].
