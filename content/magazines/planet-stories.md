*Planet Stories* was Fiction House's pulp of interplanetary adventure, published from 1939 to 1955 [1]. Its lurid
covers hid real quality: it was a home for Leigh Brackett's Martian romances, early Ray Bradbury, and Philip K. Dick's
first published story, "Beyond Lies the Wub" (1952) [1].
