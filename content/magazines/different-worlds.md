Chaosium launched *Different Worlds* in 1979, edited by Tadashi Ehara, as a magazine for all role-playing games rather
than one company's line [1]. It ran scenarios, reviews and design essays for *RuneQuest*, *Call of Cthulhu*, *Dungeons
& Dragons*, *Traveller* and others until 1987 [1].
