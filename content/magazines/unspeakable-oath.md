*The Unspeakable Oath* is a magazine for *Call of Cthulhu*, the role-playing game of H. P. Lovecraft's horrors,
founded by John Tynes and Pagan Publishing in 1990 [1]. Known for dark, adult scenarios, it introduced the *Delta
Green* setting of a secret conspiracy fighting the Mythos [1].
