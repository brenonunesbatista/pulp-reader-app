*Polyhedron* was the newszine of the RPGA, TSR's Role Playing Game Association, from 1981 [1]. It brought club news,
tournament adventures and articles to members, and in its last years (2002–2004) appeared bound back-to-back with
*Dungeon*, with mini-games of its own [1].
