*If* was launched in 1952 by James L. Quinn [1]. In the 1960s, under Frederik Pohl, it became a lively companion to
*Galaxy* and won three consecutive Hugo Awards for best magazine (1966–1968), serializing Robert A. Heinlein's *The
Moon Is a Harsh Mistress* and publishing Harlan Ellison's "I Have No Mouth, and I Must Scream" [1]. It merged into
*Galaxy* in 1974 [1].
