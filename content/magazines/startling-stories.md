*Startling Stories* was launched in 1939 as a companion to *Thrilling Wonder Stories*, built around a complete short
novel in every issue [1]. Under Sam Merwin Jr. after the war it grew more ambitious, publishing Arthur C. Clarke, Jack
Vance and Philip José Farmer's controversial "The Lovers" (1952) [1]. It ended in 1955 [1].
