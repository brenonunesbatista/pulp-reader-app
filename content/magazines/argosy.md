Frank Munsey's *Argosy*, founded in 1882, is often called the first pulp magazine: from 1896 it printed only fiction
on cheap pulp paper [1]. As a weekly it serialized Edgar Rice Burroughs, A. Merritt, Max Brand and Erle Stanley
Gardner; after 1943 it became a men's magazine, and it ran until 1978 [1].
