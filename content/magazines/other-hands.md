*Other Hands* was an international journal for role-playing in Tolkien's Middle-earth, edited by Chris Seeman from
1993 to 2001 [1]. Written by fans and scholars for Iron Crown's *Middle-earth Role Playing* and similar games, it is
known for its careful research into Tolkien's lands beyond the maps [1].
