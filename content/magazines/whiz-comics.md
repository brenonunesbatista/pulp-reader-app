*Whiz Comics* introduced Captain Marvel in its first issue, dated February 1940 (numbered 2): the boy Billy Batson
says "Shazam!" and becomes the World's Mightiest Mortal [1][2]. Created by C. C. Beck and Bill Parker, Captain Marvel
outsold Superman in the 1940s; the anthology also ran Spy Smasher, Ibis the Invincible and Golden Arrow [1].
