*Só Aventuras* ("Only Adventures") was a Brazilian series of solo adventures — interactive gamebooks played alone with
dice — presented as special editions of *Dragão Brasil* [1]. Each issue held a complete adventure, an easy way into
role-playing for new readers [1].
