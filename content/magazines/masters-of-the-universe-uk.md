Mattel's *Masters of the Universe* toys (1982) and Filmation's *He-Man* cartoon made Prince Adam of Eternia a
household name in the 1980s [1]. In Britain, London Editions published a *Masters of the Universe* comic with its own
stories from 1986, plus an *Adventure Magazine* and specials; these scans come from the fans of he-man.org [2].
