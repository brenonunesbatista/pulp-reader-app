Frank Miller's *Sin City* began in 1991 in *Dark Horse Presents*: hard-boiled crime stories set in Basin City, drawn
in stark black and white with almost no grey [1]. The seven books — from *The Hard Goodbye* to *Hell and Back* —
revived the noir of the pulps in comics, and Miller co-directed their 2005 film adaptation with Robert Rodriguez [1].
