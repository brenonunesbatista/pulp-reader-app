Launched in 1930 as *Astounding Stories of Super-Science*, the magazine was transformed by John W. Campbell, its
editor from 1937 to 1971 [1]. His "Golden Age" *Astounding* published Isaac Asimov's Foundation and robot stories,
Robert A. Heinlein, A. E. van Vogt and Theodore Sturgeon, and later serialized Frank Herbert's *Dune* [1]. Renamed
*Analog* in 1960, it is still published [1].
