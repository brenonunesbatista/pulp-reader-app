*The Complete Peanuts* reprints every *Peanuts* comic strip Charles M. Schulz drew from 1950 to 2000, two years per
volume, in a series Fantagraphics published from 2004 to 2016 [1]. Daily strips and Sunday pages follow Charlie Brown,
Snoopy and their friends through half a century [1].
