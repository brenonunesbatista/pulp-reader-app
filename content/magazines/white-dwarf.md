Games Workshop founded *White Dwarf* in June 1977, edited by Ian Livingstone, as a magazine for science fiction and
fantasy gaming [1]. Its first hundred issues covered many role-playing games — *Dungeons & Dragons*, *RuneQuest*,
*Traveller*, *Call of Cthulhu* — with famous scenarios and columns; by the late 1980s it had become the house magazine
for Games Workshop's own Warhammer games, as it still is [1].
