*Adventure*, founded in 1910, was one of the most respected pulps, especially under editor Arthur Sullivant Hoffman
(1912–1927) [1]. It published Talbot Mundy, Harold Lamb, Arthur D. Howden Smith and many others in stories of the
frontier, the sea and the East, and its letters column, "The Camp-Fire", built a loyal community of readers [1].
