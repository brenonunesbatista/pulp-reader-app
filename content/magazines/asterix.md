*Asterix* was created by writer René Goscinny and artist Albert Uderzo in the magazine *Pilote* in 1959: a small
Gaulish village holds out against Julius Caesar's Rome thanks to a magic potion [1]. Its albums, full of puns and
parodies, became one of the best-selling comics in the world; Uderzo continued alone after Goscinny's death in 1977,
and new authors took over in 2013 [1].
