*Amazing Stories* was the first magazine devoted entirely to science fiction. Hugo Gernsback launched it in April
1926, filling the early issues with reprints of Jules Verne, H. G. Wells and Edgar Allan Poe under Frank R. Paul's
colourful covers [1]. Gernsback lost it in 1929; under Ziff-Davis from 1938 it became a lively action pulp edited by
Raymond A. Palmer, and later, under Cele Goldsmith in the 1960s, a home for new writers [1]. Its letter column helped
create organised science fiction fandom [1].
