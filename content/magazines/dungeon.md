*Dungeon* began in 1986 as TSR's bimonthly magazine of ready-to-play adventures for *Dungeons & Dragons*, written
largely by readers [1]. It turned monthly under Paizo in the 2000s, when it ran ambitious campaign-length adventure
paths, and like *Dragon* moved online in 2007, where it lasted until 2013 [1].
