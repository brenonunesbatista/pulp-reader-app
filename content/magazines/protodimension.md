*Protodimension* was a free fan magazine for the role-playing games of the old GDW line — *Twilight: 2000*,
*Traveller*, *Dark Conspiracy* and others — published online from 2009 to 2016 [1]. Each issue gathered scenarios,
equipment and setting articles by fans keeping these games alive [1].
