A short-lived companion to *Isaac Asimov's Science Fiction Magazine*, *Asimov's SF Adventure Magazine* published four
issues in 1978–1979 [1]. As its name says, it leaned toward action and adventure science fiction, in a larger format
with more illustrations than its sister magazine [1].
