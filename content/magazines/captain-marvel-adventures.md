*Captain Marvel Adventures* was the hero's own title from 1941, its first issue drawn partly by a young Jack Kirby and
Joe Simon [1]. Written largely by Otto Binder and drawn by C. C. Beck, its whimsical stories made it the best-selling
comic book of the 1940s; it ended in 1953 [1][2].
