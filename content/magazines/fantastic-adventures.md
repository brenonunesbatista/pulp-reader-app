Ziff-Davis launched *Fantastic Adventures* in 1939 as a fantasy companion to *Amazing Stories*, edited by Raymond A.
Palmer [1]. It favoured light, colourful adventure — Edgar Rice Burroughs, David Wright O'Brien and Robert Bloch's
humorous Lefty Feep stories — and in 1953 was merged into *Fantastic* [1].
