Game Designers' Workshop published the *Journal of the Travellers' Aid Society* from 1979 as the official magazine of
its science fiction role-playing game *Traveller* [1]. Its 24 issues filled in the Third Imperium with library data,
adventures, ships and creatures; in 1986 it became *Challenge* [1].
