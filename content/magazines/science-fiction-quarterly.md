*Science Fiction Quarterly* was a Columbia Publications pulp that ran in two series, 1940–1943 and 1951–1958 [1]. The
first series reprinted a complete novel per issue; the second, edited by Robert W. Lowndes, published new stories by
writers such as Isaac Asimov and James Blish [1].
