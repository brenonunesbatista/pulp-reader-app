*Thrilling Wonder Stories* was Hugo Gernsback's *Wonder Stories* relaunched by Standard Magazines in 1936, aimed at
younger readers with action and bug-eyed-monster covers [1]. After the war, under Sam Merwin Jr., it became one of the
better magazines of its day, printing Ray Bradbury, Jack Vance and Arthur C. Clarke, before closing in 1955 [1].
