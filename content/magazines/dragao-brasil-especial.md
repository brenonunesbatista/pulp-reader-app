The *Dragão Brasil Especial* issues were stand-alone special editions of *Dragão Brasil*, most of them complete games
or rule sets [1]. They include *Defensores de Tóquio* — the origin of *3D&T* — and role-playing adaptations of video
and fighting games of the 1990s such as *Street Fighter* and *Mortal Kombat* [1].
