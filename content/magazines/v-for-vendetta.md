Alan Moore and David Lloyd began *V for Vendetta* in 1982 in the British anthology *Warrior*, in black and white: a
masked anarchist, V, fights a fascist Britain of the near future [1]. *Warrior* closed before the story ended; DC
Comics reprinted it in colour and published the conclusion as a ten-issue series in 1988–1989 [1]. The Guy Fawkes mask
of V later became a worldwide symbol of protest [1].
