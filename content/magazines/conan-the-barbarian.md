Marvel's *Conan the Barbarian* began in 1970, written by Roy Thomas and drawn by Barry Windsor-Smith, adapting Robert
E. Howard's sword-and-sorcery hero [1]. These first 24 issues, with Windsor-Smith's increasingly ornate art, made the
series a success and opened the way for a run of 275 issues [1].
