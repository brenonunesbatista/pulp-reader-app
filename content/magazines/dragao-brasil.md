*Dragão Brasil* was Brazil's best-known role-playing magazine, launched in 1994 [1]. Created by Marcelo Cassaro with
Rogério Saladino and J. M. Trevisan, it published adaptations, adventures and humour, and it gave birth to two
national games: *3D&T* (Defensores de Tóquio) and the fantasy world of *Tormenta* [1]. The print run ended in the
mid-2000s; the magazine returned online in 2016 [1].
