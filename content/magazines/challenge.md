*Challenge* continued the *Journal of the Travellers' Aid Society* from issue 25 in 1986, widening its scope to all of
GDW's games — *Traveller*, *Twilight: 2000*, *Space: 1889*, *Dark Conspiracy* — and to science fiction gaming in
general [1]. It ended with GDW's closure in the mid-1990s [1].
