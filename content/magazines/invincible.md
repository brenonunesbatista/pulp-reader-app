Robert Kirkman and Cory Walker's *Invincible* (Image Comics, 2003–2018) follows Mark Grayson, a teenager who inherits
the powers of his father Omni-Man, the world's greatest hero — until the father's real mission comes out [1]. Drawn
for most of its 144 issues by Ryan Ottley, it mixed classic superhero adventure with shocking violence, and became an
animated series in 2021 [1].
