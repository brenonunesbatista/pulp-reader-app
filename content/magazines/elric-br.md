Michael Moorcock's Elric, the albino sorcerer-emperor of Melniboné bound to the soul-drinking sword Stormbringer,
first appeared in *Science Fantasy* in 1961 [1]. Julien Blondel, Robin Recht, Didier Poli and Jean Bastide adapted his
saga for the French publisher Glénat from 2013; these three books are a fan translation into Brazilian Portuguese [2].
