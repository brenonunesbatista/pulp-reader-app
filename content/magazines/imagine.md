*Imagine* was TSR's British role-playing magazine, published monthly from April 1983 to 1985 (30 issues) [1].
Alongside *Dungeons & Dragons* material it covered other games, ran fiction and reviews, and developed its own fantasy
setting, the town of Pelinore [1].
