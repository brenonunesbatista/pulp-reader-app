*The Space Gamer* was started in 1975 by Howard Thompson of Metagaming Concepts as a magazine for science fiction and
fantasy games [1]. Steve Jackson Games took it over in 1980; through the early 1980s it was one of the main magazines
reviewing and discussing the new role-playing and board games of the hobby [1].
