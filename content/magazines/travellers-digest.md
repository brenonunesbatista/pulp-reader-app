*Travellers' Digest* was an independent *Traveller* magazine from Digest Group Publications, whose writers later
created *MegaTraveller* for GDW [1][2]. Its 21 issues, published in the second half of the 1980s, carried adventures —
notably the long "Grand Tour" of the Imperium — and rules and background articles [1].
