*Peter Parker, the Spectacular Spider-Man* started in December 1976 as Marvel's second monthly Spider-Man series,
alongside *The Amazing Spider-Man*, and ran until 1998 [1]. Its stories leaned toward Peter Parker's life at college
and street-level crime; issue #64 (1982) introduced Cloak and Dagger [1][2].
