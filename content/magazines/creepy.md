James Warren launched *Creepy* in 1964 as a black-and-white horror comics magazine — a format outside the Comics Code
[1]. Hosted by Uncle Creepy, it revived the spirit of the EC horror comics with artists such as Frank Frazetta, Al
Williamson, Reed Crandall and, later, Bernie Wrightson and Richard Corben [1][2].
