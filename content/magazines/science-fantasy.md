*Science Fantasy* was the fantasy-leaning British companion to *New Worlds*, edited mostly by John Carnell from 1951
[1]. It published the first Elric stories by Michael Moorcock and early work by Brian Aldiss, J. G. Ballard and Thomas
Burnett Swann [1].
