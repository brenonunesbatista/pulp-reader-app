*Turok, Son of Stone* followed two Native American brothers, Turok and Andar, trapped in a hidden valley where
dinosaurs still live [1]. Produced by Western Publishing, it began at Dell in 1954 and continued under the Gold Key
and Whitman imprints until 1982 [1].
