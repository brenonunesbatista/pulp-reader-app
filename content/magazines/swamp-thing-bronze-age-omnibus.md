This omnibus collects the original *Swamp Thing* series of 1972–1976, created by writer Len Wein and artist Bernie
Wrightson after the character's first appearance in *House of Secrets* in 1971 [1]. Wrightson's gothic art on the
first ten issues made it a horror classic [1].
