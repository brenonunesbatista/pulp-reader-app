*Galaxy Science Fiction* appeared in October 1950 under editor H. L. Gold, who wanted sophisticated, socially
satirical science fiction rather than gadget stories [1]. In its first years it serialized Alfred Bester's *The
Demolished Man* and Pohl and Kornbluth's *Gravy Planet* (*The Space Merchants*), and printed Ray Bradbury's "The
Fireman", the seed of *Fahrenheit 451* [1]. Frederik Pohl edited it through the 1960s; it closed in 1980 [1].
